# Ver2 integrated asset provenance

This release was requested by the project owner for the separate
`UDteach/AnimalsDesktopV2` repository on 2026-10-05, using the assets already
integrated in AnimalsDesktop. It is a source snapshot based on predecessor
`UDteach/AnimalsDesktop` commit `14f053190c78a9e7cd96489db7681dd06599ac16`.
The predecessor remains available with its original history and releases.

The seven timed families retain their recorded origin in
`UDteach/MofuMouse` commit `8413ac14c03773098dffa4ce760d6a7d808f5dc5` and upstream
manifest SHA256 `fdf28eea534d653f868866ddc38473aa3c3861e0fdc633e2367d2b8382893b23`.
Per-variant source, action provenance and all 1,330 PNG hashes are in
`assets/motions/manifest.json` (SHA256
`19d9a5d0c29d1a65234ef13a4337790d9d81bc6e3d3dc8f9f795fe37227a5691`).
The three-frame golden-hamster reaction has its own adoption evidence in that
manifest. The original candidate labels and historical license-review notes
are preserved as evidence of the import; they do not assert a third-party
open-source license or grant downstream redistribution rights.

The project owner's explicit request authorizes this publication. This release
does not introduce a new license. Unfinished production frames, private task
records and local generated-image caches are excluded.

Stance calibration and the bounded Windows native review referenced by the
manifest are included under `docs/motions/ver2/integration/qa/`. They are
observations from the integrated build, not proof of complete locomotion
acceptance for all 60 future motion families. Mac hardware, physical mixed-DPI
and full-cycle foot-slip measurements remain outside the completed checks.
